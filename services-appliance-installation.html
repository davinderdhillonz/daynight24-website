Benefits of Professional Installation

  • Prevents leaks and water damage: Braided steel supply lines, secured drain loops, and full test cycles before we pack up.
  • Ensures safety compliance: Correct clearances, anti-tip brackets, electrical plugs, and ductwork alignment.
  • Improves appliance lifespan: Precision levelling eliminates vibration, bearing wear, and noisy spin or wash cycles.
  • Maintains manufacturer warranty: Installed to factory guidelines so coverage remains valid.

Our Step-by-Step Process

Four steps from unboxed to tested

Pre-install assessment

We verify cabinet dimensions, water valves, power outlets, and venting before lifting the unit.

Careful placement

Floors and counters are padded while we slide your appliance cleanly into position.

Secure connections

Water lines, drain hoses, exhaust ducts, anti-tip brackets, and power cords secured tight.

Final testing

We run an initial cycle, check for zero leaks or vibration, and haul away packaging.
