Questions

Before you book

The phone is on around the clock and we book into evenings and weekends as standard. Overnight work is arranged case by case — call and tell us what you need and when, and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it.

Often same day or next day for installs and TV mounts in Edmonton. Detailing bookings usually need a little more notice because they take longer on site. Call for the soonest opening.

For an install, the appliance on site and clear access to the space. Some jobs need a specific hose, cord, vent kit or mounting bracket — we tell you exactly which one when you book, so nothing stalls halfway through. For detailing, a parking spot and access to the vehicle.

Gas ranges and gas cooktops in 30" and 36" are part of our appliance lineup. Tell us your setup and connection type when you call and we will confirm what the job involves before we schedule it.

We disconnect and move the old unit out of the way as part of the install, and the packaging goes with us. Full removal and disposal of the old appliance can be added — mention it when you book so we bring the right vehicle.

Every job is quoted before we start, and the number we give you is the number you pay unless you ask for extra work. If we discover something we could not see from the description, we stop and talk to you first.

A standard mount on a stud wall usually takes about an hour, a little longer with cable concealment or a full device setup. We will give you a realistic window when you book, not an optimistic one.
